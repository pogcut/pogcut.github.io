How to clip on Twitch

Twitch's built-in Clip Creator is the fastest way to save a moment. Here's how to make a clip, get a clean vertical version for TikTok, Shorts and Reels, and what to do when the moment runs longer than Twitch allows.

1. Make the clip (up to 60 seconds)

According to Twitch Help, you click the clip icon on a live stream or a past broadcast to open the Clip Creator. It opens as an overlay you can drag around the page, and it mutes the stream so you can hear the clip. Then:

  1. Drag the slider under the player to pick the moment, up to 60 seconds.
  2. Review the suggested crop. Twitch detects your camera and gameplay and suggests a frame; you can pick anything from landscape 16:9 to portrait 9:16.
  3. Give the clip a title and click Publish.

On mobile you may need to tap the video before the clip icon shows up.

2. Fix the vertical layout

For clips of your own channel, go to Creator Dashboard, Content, Clips, select a clip and click the scissors icon. You can re-trim, retitle, add captions, and choose a portrait layout: Stacked puts your camera over the gameplay, and Full uses one frame, which suits just-chatting or streams without a cam. Want more control over the stack? Our webcam-over-gameplay guide has a free FFmpeg command for it.

3. Share it

The Share menu can post a clip straight to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Snapchat, or copy a link for anywhere else. Streamers decide whether viewers can export their clips to social accounts. Before you post, it's worth checking the first second: a clip that starts on the setup, not three seconds before it, holds viewers. See our titles and hooks guide.

4. When the moment is longer than 60 seconds

Good stream stories often run 70 to 120 seconds. Two ways to keep the whole thing:

  • Join two clips. Make back-to-back clips, download both, and join them with FFmpeg. We tested this on two 1080x1920 60fps files; it re-encodes so the join is clean:
ffmpeg -i part1.mp4 -i part2.mp4 -filter_complex "[0:v][0:a][1:v][1:a]concat=n=2:v=1:a=1[v][a]" -map "[v]" -map "[a]" -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -preset medium -pix_fmt yuv420p -c:a aac -b:a 192k -movflags +faststart combined.mp4
  • Cut it from the VOD. Download just that section of your VOD, then crop it to vertical as in our YouTube Shorts guide. Shorts allow up to 3 minutes, and Reels under 3 minutes can still reach new viewers.

5. Clip more than you think

A single stream usually has several moments worth posting, and they're easy to miss live. Chat spikes and emote bursts in the VOD point to them; our highlight-finding guide shows how to spot them without rewatching hours of footage.
