How to post Twitch clips as YouTube Shorts

YouTube Shorts is one of the easiest places to grow a stream audience from highlights you already have. Here's how long a Short can be, how to export one from a Twitch clip, and the copyright rule that quietly blocks a lot of gaming Shorts.

1. Pick the right length

According to YouTube Help, any square or vertical video up to three minutes uploaded on or after October 15, 2024 is categorized as a Short. Three minutes is a ceiling, not a target: most stream moments land best when they get straight to the setup and end right after the payoff. If a clip needs a long story, it may work better as a regular video.

2. Watch out for music over one minute

This is the one that catches streamers. YouTube says a Short over one minute with an active copyright claim of any type is blocked globally: not playable, not recommended, not monetized. Shorts under a minute aren't affected by that rule. Game soundtracks, songs playing on stream and alert sounds can all trigger Content ID, so:

  • Keep clips with music in the background under 60 seconds.
  • For longer clips, cut or mute the music section, or pick a moment without it.
  • If a claim is wrong, you can dispute it; the Short becomes viewable again once it's resolved.

3. Export a vertical 1080x1920 file

Your stream is 16:9 and a Short should be 9:16. This free FFmpeg command takes a 59-second slice starting at 0:05, crops the center of a 1080p stream to vertical, scales it to 1080x1920 and keeps 60fps:

ffmpeg -ss 5 -i clip.mp4 -t 59 -vf "crop=ih*9/16:ih,scale=1080:1920,fps=60" -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -preset medium -pix_fmt yuv420p -c:a aac -b:a 192k -movflags +faststart short.mp4

A center crop works for full-screen gameplay or just-chatting. If you want your webcam on top and gameplay below, use the webcam-over-gameplay layout guide instead. Don't have the footage yet? See how to download your Twitch VOD.

4. Win the first second

Shorts viewers swipe fast. Start a beat before the action, put a short line of text on screen that sets up the payoff, and burn in word-by-word captions since many people watch muted. Our titles and hooks guide has templates that work for stream moments.

5. Upload it

Upload from the YouTube app or YouTube Studio. A vertical file under three minutes is treated as a Short automatically. Add your Twitch channel name in the description so viewers can find the live stream, and post the same file to TikTok and Reels too (see the VOD to TikTok guide).
