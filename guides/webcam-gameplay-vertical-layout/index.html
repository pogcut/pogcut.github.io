How to stack webcam over gameplay in a vertical Twitch clip

A plain center crop of a stream cuts off either your face or the game. The layout you see on most stream clips on TikTok and Shorts splits the screen: facecam on top, gameplay below. You can build it for free with one FFmpeg command.

1. Find the webcam rectangle

Open the clip in any player, pause on a frame, and take a screenshot. In an image editor, note where your facecam sits on the 1920x1080 frame: its left edge (x), top edge (y), width and height. A common bottom-right facecam might be 480x270 starting at x=1420, y=790. Trim tight so no game UI or overlay border sneaks in.

2. Pick the gameplay crop

Decide which part of the game matters. For shooters it's usually the center, around the crosshair. For strategy or card games you may need a wider slice. Crop it at the same shape as the panel it will fill, or it will look stretched.

3. Do the 1080x1920 math

The finished clip is 1080 pixels wide and 1920 tall. A 16:9 webcam scaled to 1080 wide is 608 tall, which leaves 1312 for gameplay. To fill a 1080x1312 panel without stretching, crop a slice of the game that is about 888x1080 from the source (1080 ÷ 1312 × 1080 ≈ 888).

  • Webcam panel: 1080x608 (top).
  • Gameplay panel: 1080x1312 (bottom), from an 888x1080 center slice starting at x=516.
  • Want a bigger game? Give the webcam less height and crop it a little wider than 16:9 instead.

4. Stack it with FFmpeg

This one command crops both parts, scales them and stacks them, keeping your original frame rate and audio:

ffmpeg -i clip.mp4 -filter_complex "[0:v]crop=480:270:1420:790,scale=1080:608[cam];[0:v]crop=888:1080:516:0,scale=1080:1312[game];[cam][game]vstack=inputs=2,setsar=1[v]" -map "[v]" -map 0:a -c:v libx264 -crf 20 -preset veryfast -c:a aac -b:a 160k clip_vertical.mp4

Swap in your own webcam numbers from step 1. The crop filter reads width:height:x:y. If you stream at 60fps, the output stays 60fps, which keeps fast games smooth on Shorts.

5. Check the safe zones on a phone

Short-video apps cover the bottom of the screen with the caption, username and buttons, and the right edge with like and share icons. Send the clip to your phone and check that the action and your face aren't hidden. Captions go best near the middle, around the line between the two panels.

No facecam? Two options

  • Single center crop: crop a 608x1080 slice from the middle and scale it to 1080x1920. Best when the action stays centered.
  • Blurred background: put a blurred, zoomed copy of the full frame behind a sharp 1080-wide copy of the whole 16:9 frame. Nothing gets cut off, at the cost of a smaller game view.
