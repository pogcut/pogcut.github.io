FAQ

How does PogCut find highlights?

We look at spikes in your Twitch chat activity, then an AI model reviews the transcript around each spike to pick moments that are actually funny or hype.

Do I need to upload my VODs?

No. You give us your Twitch channel and we pull your past broadcasts automatically. Only your channel is processed, and only after you opt in.

What format are the clips?

Vertical 9:16 video with burned-in captions, webcam on top and zoomed gameplay below, ready for TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

Who owns the clips?

You do. It's your content. We only make clips for streamers who opt in, and we never post anything to your accounts.

How much does it cost?

PogCut is in free beta. Join the waitlist for early access. Paid plans may come later, and beta users will hear about them first.

Do you support Kick or YouTube?

We're starting with Twitch. Tell us on the waitlist if you want other platforms.
